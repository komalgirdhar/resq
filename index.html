🚨 Report an Animal How RESQ Works

Three steps. One purpose.

01 — SPOT

See an animal that needs help.

Observe the situation from a safe distance. Note their condition and exact location without putting yourself or the animal in danger.

02 — REPORT

Share a photo, situation and location.

Use our simple form below. Upload a picture, select the problem, and use your phone's GPS to give us exact coordinates.

03 — RESCUE

Information reaches the rescue team.

Your detailed report is instantly routed to the relevant civic or NGO rescue teams who will use the provided maps link to reach the spot.
